### Limitations of research

The Facebook/Meta developer pages returned HTTP 400 in this environment, so no claim is made about Meta API permissions. Doppel and Bolster pages returned access errors. Vendor pages were not used as independent performance evidence. No customer interviews, source-contract review, labelled corpus, app implementation, or product benchmark was available.

### Naming research limitation

`Cairnwise` is the recommended provisional working name because its marker/navigation metaphor fits evidence, coverage, and analyst workflow without promising autonomous protection. It has not been cleared. Search-engine results, an HTTP 404, or an unreachable domain do not establish that a name is available or safe to use. See `NAMING_SHORTLIST.md` for the screening rubric and backups.
