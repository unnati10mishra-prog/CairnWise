# Cairnwise Naming Shortlist

**Decision:** use **Cairnwise** as the provisional working name in the blueprint and evaluation materials.

**Descriptor:** *Cairnwise — Evidence-first digital identity risk operations*

## What this decision means

Cairnwise is a working label, not a cleared brand. The name is compatible with the product metaphor: a cairn is a marker or navigation aid, which fits the identity registry, evidence trail, coverage markers, and analyst review path. It does not imply complete visibility, legal verification, autonomous protection, successful takedown, or authoritative attribution.

No trademark, company-name, domain, social-handle, search, or other availability conclusion is made here. The current evidence is only preliminary web probing recorded as `R31` in `RESEARCH_REGISTER.md`.

## Screening rubric

Score each candidate from 1–5 and record the reason:

- product fit with identity baselines, evidence, coverage, review, and human decisions;
- non-overclaiming language;
- immediate comprehension with the descriptor;
- memorability and spelling;
- pronunciation across intended markets;
- distinctiveness without assuming legal availability;
- extensibility to social profiles, apps, domains, ads, and future sources;
- enterprise credibility across security, fraud, brand, and legal teams;
- international, cultural, slang, and negative-association checks;
- operational phrases such as “Create a Cairnwise case” and “Cairnwise evidence export”;
- search-result confusion and unrelated uses;
- professional trademark, company-name, domain, and social-handle clearance.

Use **40/55 or higher** as a reason to continue testing, not as legal approval. A candidate below 33 should normally be rejected or retained only as a research backup.

## Required pre-launch checks

1. Run a professional trademark search in the intended jurisdictions and relevant classes.
2. Check company-name and registered-business conflicts in launch markets.
3. Check exact and confusingly similar domains through an accredited registrar.
4. Check social handles, app-store names, and package/bundle naming conflicts.
5. Test pronunciation and comprehension with at least five target users.
6. Re-check conflicts immediately before public launch because availability changes.

Until these checks are complete, all documents and demos must use language such as **“Cairnwise is a provisional working name.”**
