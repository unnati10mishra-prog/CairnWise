01 / The operating problem

A similar name is a signal.It is not a verdict.

External identity risk sits between brand, fraud, security, and legal teams. Cairnwise gives them a shared, defensible place to decide what deserves attention.

resemblanceauthorizationpotential harmevidence quality
When an account or app appears to use your identity, the hard question is not “does this look familiar?” It is “what was actually observed, what is authorized, and what is still missing?”

Designed for teams protecting high-trust brands across social profiles and app stores. The workflow keeps resemblance, authorization, potential harm, and evidence quality separate.

See the evidence model
02 / The workflow

From official baseline toaccountable recheck.

A focused workflow instead of a noisy alert feed. Each stage leaves behind a record an analyst can understand, review, and export.

01

Define the official baseline.

Register the brand name, logo variants, official social accounts, mobile apps, publishers, aliases, regions, partners, and approved exceptions.

Identity registryVersioned
02

Observe permitted sources.

Capture what a connector can actually see, when it saw it, how it was collected, and where the source is unavailable, stale, rate-limited, or unsupported.

Coverage contractProvenance
03

Compare explainably.

Keep name, logo, description, publisher, stable identifier, and external-link signals distinct. Similarity can prioritize a review; it cannot decide intent.

Deterministic signalsNo black box
04

Review with uncertainty.

Show resemblance, authorization, potential harm, and evidence quality side by side, with counterevidence and a clear next action.

Human decisionAbstention
05

Act, then recheck.

Prepare an evidence-linked report draft for approval. Track submitted, acknowledged, removed observed, reappeared, disputed, and unknown as separate outcomes.

Outcome ledgerAuditable

05 / Coverage, without theatre

Know what the sourcecouldn’t tell you.

“No records” is not the same as “unavailable.” Cairnwise makes source health a first-class part of every review, with operational, degraded, unavailable, unsupported, stale, and not evaluated states.

01

Similarity never equals intent.

Name, logo, or description resemblance can create a review candidate. It cannot prove impersonation or malicious intent.

02

Abstention is safer than certainty.

Missing or contradictory evidence routes a case to insufficient evidence instead of forcing a verdict.

03

Action stays human-approved.

Report drafts can be prepared from the ledger. Consequential submission remains with an authorized reviewer.
