Digital Risk Protection – Social & App Monitoring
1. Background
Brands are increasingly impersonated across social media platforms and mobile app stores. Threat actors can create fake company profiles, scam accounts, fraudulent pages, and malicious or unauthorized mobile applications that imitate legitimate brands.

These impersonations can be used to deceive customers, distribute scams, steal credentials, damage brand reputation, or redirect users to fraudulent services. Organizations need an efficient way to discover and prioritize these threats across external social and app ecosystems.

2. Challenge Statement
Design and build a Digital Risk Protection platform focused specifically on Social & App Monitoring.

The platform should help organizations identify potential:

Fake or impersonating social-media profiles
Fake company or brand pages
Scam accounts using a company's identity
Fake or suspicious mobile applications impersonating a brand
3. Core Scope
Brand Profile
Allow an organization to define legitimate assets such as:

Company/brand name
Official logo
Official social-media accounts
Official mobile applications
Other relevant brand identifiers
Social Media Monitoring
Identify potential:

Brand impersonation accounts
Fake company pages
Scam profiles
Accounts using similar names, logos, or branding
App Store Monitoring
Identify suspicious applications using:

Similar app names
Brand/logo impersonation
Different developers or publishers
App descriptions or branding resembling the legitimate company
4. Bonus Features
Look-alike Name Detection
Detect common name variations such as:

Character swaps
Added words
Spacing changes
The detection should avoid over-flagging legitimate or normal names.

5. Deliverables
Working prototype covering brand profile, social media monitoring, and app store monitoring.
Architecture diagram covering data sources, detection logic, storage, and how official assets are excluded.
Short demo or a few slides showing the approach.
6. Evaluation Criteria — 100 Points
Criterion	Weight	What Judges Look For
Social Media Monitoring	30%	Detection of impersonating profiles, fake pages, and scam accounts using similar names, logos, or branding; official accounts should not be flagged
App Store Monitoring	30%	Detection of suspicious apps using similar names, logos, publisher differences, and resembling descriptions; official apps should not be flagged
Brand Profile	15%	Clear definition of legitimate assets, validation, and use of the profile to guide detection
User Experience	15%	Easy setup, readable detections, and graceful handling of unavailable sources
Presentation & Demo	10%	Working prototype demonstrated clearly through a short demo or a few slides

Look-alike Name Detection → Social Media Monitoring & App Store Monitoring
Bonus credit is capped at approximately 5–10 points in total.
